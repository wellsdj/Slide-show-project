Chapter 01
What Is House?

House music is an electronic dance genre born in Chicago's underground clubs. It fuses the soul of disco, the groove of funk, and the pulse of synthesizers into one unstoppable four-on-the-floor format.

The name comes from the Warehouse, a legendary Chicago club where DJ Frankie Knuckles spun records in the early 1980s. The music that played there became known as "house."

Chapter 02
The History
1977
The Warehouse Opens DJ Frankie Knuckles begins spinning disco and electronic tracks on Chicago's South Side.
1984
First House Records Jesse Saunders & Vince Lawrence release "On and On" — widely credited as the first house record.
1987
House Goes Global UK embraces house. Acid house explodes with the Roland TB-303 bassline sound.
NOW
Still Alive Tech house, afro house, melodic house — the genre keeps evolving, dancefloors keep moving.
Chapter 03
The Structure

Every house track is built on the same skeleton — four layers stacked on a relentless four-on-the-floor grid:

🥁
Kick
Hits on every beat. The heartbeat of the track — punchy, deep, relentless.
🎸
Bass
Synth or 303 bass locks in with the kick. Carries the groove and low-end warmth.
🎹
Lead
Melody, chords, stabs. The emotional hook that makes you remember the track.
🎵
Full Mix
Everything together — the complete house experience from intro to drop.
